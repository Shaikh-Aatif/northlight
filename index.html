    About the work shown here

    AZURE & STONE and HALCYON are self-initiated concept projects. Both properties are invented — there is no such resort and no such estate, and the names, prices, rates and figures inside them were written for the demonstration. They are not client commissions and no client is implied.

    All imagery in both projects, and on this page, was generated with AI and depicts no real building or location.
